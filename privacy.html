Privacy policy

King of Wordor, by RamTek · Effective 20 October 2026 (previous version: 11 October 2026)

In short

You can play the whole game as a guest, without giving a name or an email. On the first launch the game creates an anonymous game account for you, so that your progress - rank, crowns, boosters and records - is kept on our server and is not lost with the phone. If you sign in with Google, that same account is linked to your Google account: we then also store your email address and name, your scores appear on the public ladders, and your progress follows you to a new phone. Ads are shown by Google AdMob. You can delete everything at any time.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • We do not read your contacts, location, photos or microphone.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase — database and accounts, for every player.
  • Google — Google sign-in, Google Play (purchases) and AdMob (ads).

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Delete it in the app - signed in: You → Delete account and data; as a guest: You → Delete my data - or, if you signed in with Google, on the web at delete-account.html, without reinstalling. Deletion is immediate in the live database; encrypted backups expire within 7 days. A guest account has no name or email, so once the app is uninstalled it can no longer be reached from your phone: delete it in the app first, or email us.

Children

King of Wordor is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has signed in, email us and we will delete the account.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date. Questions or requests: kingofwordor@gmail.com.
