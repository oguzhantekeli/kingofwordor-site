Privacy policy

King of Wordor, by RamTek · Effective 11 October 2026

In short

You can play the whole game as a guest. As a guest nothing about you leaves your phone. If you sign in with Google, we store what the online features need: your account, a display name and the rounds you post to the ladders. Ads are shown by Google AdMob. You can delete everything at any time.

What we collect, and why

DataWhenWhy
Progress, settings, scores, coins and boostersAlwaysStored only on your phone, so the game works offline. Not sent to us.
Google account identifier, email address and nameOnly if you sign inTo create your account. Provided by Google sign-in and stored by our backend provider, Supabase.
Display name and house coloursOnly if you sign inShown next to your score on the public ladders and to duel opponents.
Rounds you post: the words you played, their timing, score, language and dateOnly if you sign inTo check the score is real (the server replays the round) and to rank the ladders and duels.
Advertising identifier, device and usage informationWhen an ad is shownCollected by Google AdMob to show ads and measure them. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.
Purchase recordOnly if you buy somethingPayments are handled by Google Play. We receive a purchase confirmation, never your payment details.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • We do not read your contacts, location, photos or microphone.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase — database and sign-in for signed-in players.
  • Google — Google sign-in, Google Play (purchases) and AdMob (ads).

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Delete it in the app (Profile → Delete account and data) or on the web at delete-account.html, without reinstalling. Deletion is immediate in the live database; encrypted backups expire within 7 days. Guest data lives only on your phone: uninstalling the app removes it.

Children

King of Wordor is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has signed in, email us and we will delete the account.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date. Questions or requests: kingofwordor@gmail.com.
